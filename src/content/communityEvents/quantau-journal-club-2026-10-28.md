## A place to discuss quantum research

The opening session is **planned for 28 October 2026**. The proposed journal-club format includes refreshments at **12:30**, followed by talks from **13:00 to 14:00**, in Israel time.

The recurring format under discussion is a meeting every two weeks, with two approximately 20-minute talks. The opening session was proposed as an invited talk.

**The final format, opening speaker, venue, and schedule are not yet confirmed.** Discussions about combining the journal club with the seminar series are ongoing. Further recurring dates are therefore not listed as confirmed events.
